Warehouse Management takes ERP's stock down to the bin: which pallet, lot and owner is where, what it
is doing and who is responsible for it. It runs receiving through returns, with people and robots on
one task list, and it never keeps a second balance: a warehouse's positions always add up to ERP's
stock, which the database itself enforces.

A robot's "mission complete" is not stock moved. A move finishes when the right unit is confirmed at
the right place, and until then its custody is said out loud.
