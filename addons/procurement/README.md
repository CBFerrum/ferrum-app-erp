# Procurement

From what is needed to what was ordered, and how each supplier did.

- **Material requests** say what is needed, where and by when, and each line keeps what has been
  ordered and received against it. Purchase requests become purchase orders grouped by each item's
  preferred supplier, manufacture requests work orders, transfers and issues stock entries.
- **Re-order levels** per item and warehouse raise requests when projected stock falls below them.
- **Requests for quotation** reach each supplier with a signed link to a page where they answer.
  **Supplier quotations** are priced offers, valid until a day and never ordered twice.
- **Supplier scorecards** score each month or quarter by weighted criteria, and the latest standing
  warns about, or refuses, new requests and orders to a supplier.
- Suppliers see their requests for quotation, orders and bills on the workspace's portal.
- **Reports**: open requests by item and warehouse, each request line's trail through quotation,
  order and receipt, quotations side by side, scorecard ranking and purchase analytics.
