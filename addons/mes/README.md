Manufacturing Execution runs work orders on the floor. A run starts only against an approved revision,
with its material there, its equipment in service and a qualified operator. What actually happened is
recorded as events, so yield, scrap and rework are facts rather than a closing figure, and every lot
that went into a product can be followed in both directions.

It dispatches an approved job to a controller. It never writes controller logic, and machine sequencing
and safety stay with the controller.
